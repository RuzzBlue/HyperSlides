Prepare a Wallet Safely

Set up in a quiet place and use a small test balance. Your recovery phrase is the master backup — only you should ever see a real one.

Type or use a trusted bookmark for the official wallet site or app store listing.

What to submit for the lab

Only proof that does not reveal a real seed or private key: a redacted screen, public transaction URL, written reflection, or confirmation.
