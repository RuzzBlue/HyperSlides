Hot Wallets vs Cold Storage

Wallets manage keys; they do not hold coins the way a physical wallet holds cash. Choose storage based on risk, amount, and how often you move funds.

Connected

Hot wallet

Internet-connected software. Convenient for learning and everyday amounts — but exposed to phishing and malware.

Offline keys

Cold storage

Keys remain offline, usually on dedicated hardware. Stronger isolation — still dependent on a safe backup.

Safe setup sequence

Does cold storage remove all risk?

No. It reduces online exposure, but phishing, lost backups, and mistaken transaction approvals remain real risks.
