Hot Wallets vs Cold Storage

Choose the right tool for the job: everyday spending vs long-term savings. This course’s lab focuses on a hot wallet — convenient, connected, and great for learning.

Hot wallet

Software on phone/desktop, connected to the internet (e.g. browser extension or mobile app).

  • Fast for daily use & dApps
  • Higher exposure to phishing/malware
  • Ideal for small “working” balances

Cold storage

Keys kept offline — hardware devices or air-gapped setups.

  • Stronger protection for large holdings
  • Slightly more friction to transact
  • Still requires careful seed backup
