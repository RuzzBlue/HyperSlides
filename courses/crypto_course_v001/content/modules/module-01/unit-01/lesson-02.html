Keys, Addresses & Ownership

Your private key proves ownership. Your public address is where others send funds. Lose the key, lose the coins — share the address freely.

Private key / seed phrase

Secret. Never share. Often a 12–24 word recovery phrase that recreates your wallet.

Public address

Safe to share — like an account number. Derived from your keys; used to receive crypto.

// Mental model
seedPhrase → wallet keys → address
// Anyone can send TO an address
// Only the key holder can send FROM it
Flip card · Why “not your keys, not your coins”?
If an exchange or app holds your keys, you hold an IOU. Self-custody means you control the signing key — and you alone can authorize transfers.
Pro tip: Write your seed phrase offline. Never photograph it, paste it into a website, or store it only in cloud notes.
