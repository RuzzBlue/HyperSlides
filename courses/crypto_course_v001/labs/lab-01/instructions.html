Goal

Stand up a real hot wallet for learning. Prefer a well-known wallet (Phantom, MetaMask, or similar) from the official site or app store — never from a random DM link.

Steps

  1. Download/install the wallet from the official source.
  2. Create a new wallet and write down the seed phrase offline.
  3. Confirm you can view your receive address.
  4. (Optional) Send a tiny test amount from an exchange or another wallet, then capture proof.
Safety: Use only funds you can afford to lose while learning. CryptoHub never asks for your seed phrase.
