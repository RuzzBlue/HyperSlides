Receive address

Open the receive screen. Copy your address. Compare the first and last characters before you share it (even with yourself on another device).

Verification drill

  1. Open Receive / Deposit
  2. Copy the address
    • Paste it into a temporary notes field you control
    • Compare start + end characters with the on-screen address
  3. Optionally scan the QR with a second device you own
  4. Clear the temporary notes field when finished

Evidence ideas (redacted)

  • Screenshot of the receive screen with middle characters covered
  • A short written note confirming you compared start/end characters

When you are ready, open Submit and check off the rubric steps.
