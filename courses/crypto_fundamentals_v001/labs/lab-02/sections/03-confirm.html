Confirm & reflect

Use the wallet or a reputable explorer to see the pending and confirmed status. Submit a public transaction URL or a redacted screenshot, then note which check gave you the most confidence.

Confirmation steps

  1. Open activity / history in the wallet
    • Find the outbound test transfer
    • Note pending vs confirmed state
  2. Optionally open a public explorer link
    • Verify network matches what you selected
    • Confirm amount looks right
  3. Write one reflection sentence about your strongest check

Reflection prompts

  • Which field were you most careful with?
  • What would you double-check next time under time pressure?
  • Did the explorer view add confidence or confusion?
